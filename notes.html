about me

some info about my practice.

hi, i'm lily. i'm a self-taught artist based in the UK, working across painting, mixed media, and poetry.

i like to create images that blur the boundaries between the body, landscape, reality, and imagination.

my recent work explores dream logic, memory, and perception through intuitive-led processes and unconventional materials.

i'm always exploring new ideas and developing my practice along the way.
so if any of my work resonates with you, i'd love to hear from you sometime @lei_lorei

thanks for checking out my page :)
