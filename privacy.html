privacy policy

This privacy policy explains how Lei Lorei handles information associated with purchases made through the Lei Lorei shop.

information we process

When you place an order, information required to process and fulfil your purchase may be collected through the checkout process. This may include your name, email address, shipping address, phone number, order details, and payment-related information.

Payment information is processed by Stripe. lei lorei does not directly handle or store your full payment card details.

order fulfilment

Order and delivery information required to fulfil your purchase is passed to Prodigi, our print and fulfilment provider. Prodigi uses this information to produce and deliver your order.

Prodigi may also provide Lei Lorei with information relating to the status of your order and shipment, such as fulfilment status, carrier information, and tracking information when available.

third-party services

The Lei Lorei shop uses third-party services to process payments, operate the checkout system, and fulfil orders.

Payments are processed by Stripe and orders are fulfilled through Prodigi. These services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

data use

Information associated with an order is used only as necessary to process payment, fulfil and deliver the order, communicate about the order, and maintain the shop's records.

contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the Lei Lorei shop through the contact details provided on the website.
