privacy policy

This privacy policy explains how lei lorei handles information associated with purchases made through the lei lorei shop.

information we process

When you place an order, payment and customer information required to process and fulfil the order is handled through Stripe.

gmail integration

The lei lorei shop uses the Gmail API to send order notifications to the shop owner.

The Gmail integration requests only permission to send email (gmail.send). It does not read, modify, or delete emails in the connected Gmail account.

google user data

Google account authorization is used solely to allow the shop backend to send order notification emails. Google account data obtained through the Gmail API is not sold or used for advertising.

third-party services

Payments are processed by Stripe and email notifications are sent through Google's Gmail API. These services process information according to their own privacy policies.

contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the lei lorei shop through the contact details provided on the website.
