THE PAGES.
Preface

In my nightgarden
 there are no skies, no birds,
no butterflies, no chirping or humming.

only nettles
huge, towering nettles.
 they are black, i think.

like totems, leviathan, angels of the night
 ivy, inky, and enthralling
burning deeply in my mind

from the fat of my tissue
 whitby jet ribbons unfurling and curling
like inchworms writhing and squirming
fuller, pump, plumper every day

sick and sweet in my image.


26th august 2026.
