billets doux

02

Nightshade, 2026

tap to fully load

down by the riverbed,

sand waves
shuffle under our heels.

we wander
naked and blind.

no seeing,
no perceiving,
no apprehending.

yet i can still feel
the murmurs of fish
vibrate through the reeds.

a strange sensation,
coloured in ochre and nightshade,
soon wraps around us.

tiny mouths,
searching for sunlight to feed on,
race past in thousands.

somewhere upstream,
the water grows warm.

…

out of the gloom,
springs a flesh cocoon.

ochre and nightshade
me and you

31st july 2026.
